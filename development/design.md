# Design System Specification
 
## Visual Philosophy
This design system prioritizes a clean, functional, and systematic aesthetic. By utilizing a "fidelity" color approach with a neutral primary base, the interface maintains a high level of clarity while using vibrant secondary and tertiary accents to drive user attention and signify status.
 
## Color Palette
The system operates in **Light Mode**, focusing on high legibility and a professional atmosphere.
 
*   **Primary (Foundation):** #e9ecef. A subtle, soft gray used for primary surfaces and low-impact interactive states, creating a "minimalist/ghost" aesthetic.
*   **Secondary (Action):** #e6a10f. A bold amber used for important secondary actions, highlights, and warnings.
*   **Tertiary (Success/Accent):** #00c417. A vibrant green utilized for success states, growth indicators, and decorative accents.
*   **Neutral:** Derived from the #1978e5 seed to ensure harmonious grays across backgrounds and borders.
 
## Typography
The system uses **Inter** as a variable font family across all roles to ensure maximum readability and a modern, neutral tone.
 
*   **Headlines:** Inter. Clean, geometric, and professional.
*   **Body Text:** Inter. Optimized for long-form reading and interface clarity.
*   **Labels:** Inter. Precise and legible even at small scales.
 
## Layout and Geometry
*   **Roundedness:** Level 2 (Moderate). UI components feature standard rounded corners, providing a friendly yet structured feel that balances playfulness with professional rigor.
*   **Spacing:** Level 2 (Normal). A balanced spatial rhythm that provides enough room for breathing without sacrificing information density.
 
## Component Behavior
Buttons and containers should utilize the moderate rounding (Level 2) and follow the standard spacing scale to maintain consistency across the application. Interaction states should leverage the secondary amber and tertiary green for clear feedback loops.
